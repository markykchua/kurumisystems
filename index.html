How It Works

Three Simple Steps

No setup, no accounts, no configuration. Just install and go.

1

Install the Extension

Add Kurumi to Chrome in one click from the Chrome Web Store. It's free and takes just a few seconds.

2

Chat Like You Always Do

Use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok as usual. Kurumi quietly creates a map of your conversation in a side panel.

3

Jump to Any Part Instantly

Click any bookmark to scroll right to that point. Your bookmarks are saved automatically and persist across sessions.
